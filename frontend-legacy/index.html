Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Transparent policies for your peace of mind.

How does the AI Agent plan and book my catering? +
Our AI agent is powered by an ultra-fast LLM integrated with a Spring Boot backend. It understands your guest count, cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, calculates tiered volume discounts, and confirms your order straight into our persistent SQL database.
What volume discounts are available for large events? +
We offer automatic tiered volume discounts: 5% off for 100+ guests, 10% off for 250+ guests, and 15% VIP discount for 500+ guests. You can also enter promotional codes like JUCAS2026 for an additional 5% discount.
Can we arrange a food tasting session before confirming? +
Yes! For all bookings of 100+ guests, our executive chef team hosts an exclusive complimentary tasting session for up to 4 family members at our central tasting studio.
Where are our catering bookings stored? +
All bookings are saved in our persistent Spring Boot H2 SQL database. You can review all confirmed orders by clicking the "🔐 Admin Orders" button in the footer.
